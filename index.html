About

Marko Manriquez is an MLA I candidate at Harvard GSD who treats drones, data, and digital fabrication as design materials. Before Harvard, he spent a decade building interdisciplinary programs at the School of Visual Arts. He is also a graduate of NYU ITP with a foundation in creative technologies. His work now traverses the spectrum of computational and speculative design—deploying GIS, photogrammetry, and ML-based scenario forecasting to explore adjacent futures. He's equally invested in climate tech ventures toward climate adaptation and mitigation.
